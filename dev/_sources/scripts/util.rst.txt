Utilities
=========

Tools for setting FastSurfer up, preparing inputs and working with results: downloading the network checkpoints,
conforming images, computing statistics, comparing two runs, visualizing corpus callosum results, and preparing
longitudinal subjects for FreeSurfer's tools.
